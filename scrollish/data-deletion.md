# Data Deletion — Scrollish

**Last Updated:** September 3, 2026 · Corresponds to Privacy Policy version 1.1

Scrollish (by Uplyfire) stores your learning data — your progress, streaks, saved cards, achievements, settings, and the optional name you choose — **locally on your device**. We do not keep this data on our servers and we do not maintain user accounts.

The only information that leaves your device is (a) the schedule for the optional reminder notifications you turn on, (b) analytics and crash diagnostics processed by Google Firebase (with your consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland; enabled by default elsewhere), and (c) your device's IP address whenever the App connects to Google. This page explains how to delete all of it.

## How to delete your on-device data

1. **Delete all data in-app:** Open **Settings → "Delete all data"** and confirm in the dialog. This permanently deletes all your progress, streaks, XP, achievements, saved cards, reminders and settings, and your optional name, from the device.
   ⚠️ Note: "Delete all data" clears everything stored on your device. It does not by itself delete the reminder-scheduling record described below — to remove that, turn reminders off in **Settings → Notifications** (before or after the wipe), or email us.
2. **Clear all app data:** Go to Android Settings → Apps → Scrollish → Storage → Clear Data.
3. **Uninstall the App:** Removing the App deletes all locally stored data from your device (the App excludes its data from Android's automatic backup).

## How to delete your reminder-scheduling record

If you enabled daily reminders, a minimal record (a push token, your time zone, and your chosen reminder times, keyed to an anonymous identifier — **no name, no learning data, no email**) is stored on our reminder backend in the European Union.

- **Turn reminders off:** In **Settings → Notifications**, disable reminders. This instructs our backend to delete the record for your device.
- **Uninstall the App:** Uninstalling stops reminders but does **not** by itself delete the backend record. To delete it, turn reminders off (Settings → Notifications) **before** uninstalling, or email us (below) and we will delete it.

## Data managed by third parties

- **Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics (Google):** Collected only where analytics is enabled. To stop collection, use **Settings → "Privacy & ad settings"** (shown in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) or email us. If you email us, we will delete the analytics and crash data associated with your app installation using Firebase's data-deletion tools, and confirm when it is done. Google's own controls are at [Google Account — Data & Privacy](https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy).
- **Google Play:** Subscription and purchase history is managed by Google Play. Visit [Google Play Order History](https://play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory).
