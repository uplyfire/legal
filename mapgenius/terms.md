## 2. Description of the App

MapGenius is an educational geography quiz application that may include some or all of the following features, depending on the version and available updates:

- Interactive map-based quizzes (Genius Mode), which may cover topics such as countries, capitals, flags, other geographic features, and the states and regions of individual countries
- Study modes for learning geographic information
- Daily challenges (Section 2.2)
- Global rankings and a player profile with a nickname and a country flag (Section 3)
- Progress tracking with scores, stars, and statistics
- Various difficulty levels and continent filters
- An optional daily reminder notification

Features may be added, modified, or removed over time. The specific features available to you depend on the version of the App you have installed and whether you have purchased MapGenius Pro.

### 2.1 Free Version and MapGenius Pro

The App is available in a free version (with advertisements) and with a premium upgrade ("MapGenius Pro") available through a one-time in-app purchase (Section 4).

### 2.2 Daily Challenges

The App offers daily challenges, currently the Daily Quiz, the Daily Map and the Daily Duel. They run on our servers, so they need an internet connection. In the free version, one new Daily Quiz, one new Daily Map and one new Daily Duel become available each day, in a fixed order, and days you skip do not accumulate; earlier challenges can be replayed. The day changes at midnight in the time zone recorded when your Player ID was created (Section 3.1).

For the rankings, the first attempt at each Daily Quiz and Daily Duel counts, and on the Daily Map your best score on each map counts. A ranked attempt that you end early with "Finish" is scored with the questions you answered; a ranked attempt that you abandon and do not finish may be recorded with zero points. Replays do not change the ranked result of a Daily Quiz or Daily Duel.

Daily challenge content, schedules, limits and scoring may change over time. We do not guarantee that a particular challenge will be available on a particular day.

### 2.3 Daily Reminder

If you allow notifications, the App may show one reminder a day in the evening. You can switch it off at any time under Settings, or in your device's notification settings.

## 3. Player ID, Player Profile and Rankings

### 3.1 No Account; Your Player ID

MapGenius does **not** require account creation, registration, or login, and no password, email address, or other credentials are required to use the App.

To run the daily challenges and the rankings, the App automatically creates an anonymous identifier for your installation (the "Player ID", shown under Settings → Data). Your daily challenge progress, your ranking entries and your player profile are stored on our servers under this Player ID. It is tied to the installation of the App on your device, not to you or to your Google account. If you uninstall the App or clear its data, the App creates a new Player ID, and the progress, results and ranking entries stored under the old one can no longer be reached from the App and cannot be transferred to the new one. Your MapGenius Pro purchase is not affected, because it is restored through Google Play (Section 4.5).

How we handle the data stored under your Player ID is described in our [Privacy Policy](https://uplyfire.github.io/legal/mapgenius/privacy-policy).

### 3.2 Nickname and Country

Every player has a public nickname. It starts as a generated one (for example "Genius-7K4M"); you may choose your own under Settings → Player profile, subject to the limits shown there (currently 3 to 16 characters, one change every 24 hours). You may also show a country flag next to your nickname, or choose to show none.

Your nickname must not:

- be offensive, hateful, discriminatory, threatening, sexual, or otherwise inappropriate for a general audience including teenagers
- impersonate another person, MapGenius or Uplyfire, or suggest that you represent us
- contain personal information, such as your real full name, a phone number, an address, or a social media account
- advertise or promote anything, or infringe the rights of others

You are responsible for the nickname you choose.

### 3.3 Content Moderation and Reporting Nicknames

The nickname is the only content players can publish in the App. To keep it within the rules in Section 3.2:

- **Automated check:** when you set a nickname, our server checks it against a list of offensive words and rejects it if it matches. No other automated decisions are made about nicknames
- **Review on report:** we review reported nicknames manually
- **Measures:** if a nickname breaks Section 3.2, we may replace it with a generated one. If a player repeatedly breaks the rules, or breaks Section 3.4, we may also remove their results from the rankings or exclude their Player ID from the rankings, temporarily or permanently. Such a player can still play the App
- **How to report:** email uplyfire@gmail.com with the nickname, the ranking where you saw it, and why you think it breaks these Terms or the law (a screenshot helps). If you believe the nickname is illegal, please say so and explain why
- **Statement of reasons and complaints:** we hold no contact details for players, so we cannot notify you individually when we act on your nickname or results. If your nickname was reset or your results were removed, you can email us with your Player ID to learn the reason and to contest the decision. A person, not an automated system, will review your complaint and reply

These rules are part of our obligations as a provider of a hosting service under the EU Digital Services Act. Our point of contact under that Act is uplyfire@gmail.com.

### 3.4 Fair Play and Rankings

The rankings are provided for fun and comparison only. They carry no prizes and no monetary or other value, and a place in a ranking gives you no rights.

Our servers check submitted results and may reject results that are technically impossible or that do not match the challenge. We may correct, recalculate or remove results, and rebuild the rankings, for example after fixing an error or after detecting cheating. We do not guarantee the accuracy, completeness or continuous availability of the rankings.
